MeetingFlash is a post-meeting execution tool. The problem it solves: meetings end and the real work — writing the recap, chasing who-owns-what, drafting the follow-up email — eats 20+ minutes and often never gets done properly.

Paste your raw meeting notes (bullet points, fragments, half-sentences) and in under 20 seconds MeetingFlash produces a structured Execution Pack: a client-ready follow-up email, action items with owners and deadlines, the decisions that were made (with rationale), open and inferred questions, risks with mitigations, a Slack-ready team message, and a strategic agenda for the next meeting.

What makes it different from pasting notes into ChatGPT: zero prompt-writing (the tool already knows the structure), and persistent project memory — when you tie meetings to a project, each new recap cross-references prior decisions and open tasks. That's context ChatGPT doesn't have.

Built solo, no VC. Free plan gives 5 packs per month; Pro is $12/month for unlimited packs, project memory, smart search, multi-language output (EN/FR/ES/DE), and PDF export.
